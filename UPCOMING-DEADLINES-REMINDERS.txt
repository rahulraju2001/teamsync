UPCOMING DEADLINES AND DUE-SOON REMINDERS

Dashboard
- Adds an Upcoming Deadlines section for active tasks and projects.
- Defaults to My deadlines; tasks assigned to the signed-in user and projects owned by that user are shown.
- Supports All workspace deadlines, next 7/14/30 days, task-only/project-only/all, and task-priority filters.
- Completed tasks and projects are excluded. Overdue items remain represented by the existing overdue KPI and notification system.

Reminder schedule
- Task reminders: 3 days before, 1 day before, and on the due date.
- Project reminders: 3 days before, 1 day before, and on the deadline.
- Task recipients: assignee and task creator (duplicates are removed; actor is not relevant for system reminders).
- Project recipients: project owner and workspace Administrators.
- Completed tasks/projects are skipped. Existing overdue notifications continue to run.
- The existing scheduler performs an immediate scan at startup and scans every six hours. A reminder is delivered on the first scan within its calendar-day window, not at an exact clock time.
- APP_TIME_ZONE controls calendar-day calculations and defaults to Asia/Kolkata.

User preferences
- Settings > Notifications has a due-soon reminder toggle and individual 3-day, 1-day, and due-date switches.
- Preferences are stored per user in notificationPreferences.dueSoon.
- In-app notifications must also be enabled for a reminder to be persisted/delivered.
- Email preference remains a saved preference only; email delivery is not configured.

Deduplication and changes
- Each reminder event is deduplicated per recipient, item, deadline date, and reminder window using Notification.eventKey.
- Changing a deadline creates a new reminder identity for the new date. Previously delivered notifications remain in history.
- The scheduler does not change task/project status.
