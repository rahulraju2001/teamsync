UPCOMING DEADLINES AND BASIC REMINDERS

- The dashboard includes upcoming task and project deadlines.
- Completed tasks and projects are excluded from reminders.
- Task reminders go to the assignee and task creator.
- Project reminders go to the project owner and workspace Administrators.
- Fixed due-soon reminders are sent one day before and on the due date/deadline.
- Overdue tasks and projects generate one overdue notification per deadline value and recipient.
- The scheduler scans immediately at startup and every six hours. Reminders are delivered on the first scan in the relevant calendar-day window, not at an exact clock time.
- Reminder event keys prevent duplicate notifications.
- Users do not configure reminder preferences. In-app notifications are always enabled.
- The scheduler does not change task/project status.
- APP_TIME_ZONE defaults to Asia/Kolkata.
