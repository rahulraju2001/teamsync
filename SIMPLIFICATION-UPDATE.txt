TeamSync simplification update

Notifications
- Kept MongoDB-persisted in-app notifications and Socket.IO live delivery.
- Kept automatic task and project deadline reminders.
- Reminders are fixed at one day before the deadline and on the deadline date; overdue notifications are also sent.
- Removed per-user notification preferences and the unused email-notification preference.
- Duplicate prevention remains in place for scheduled reminders.

Workspace ownership
- Removed workspace ownership transfer from the UI, frontend context, and API.
- The workspace creator remains the fixed owner and an Administrator.
- Only the owner can delete the workspace.
- Owners cannot leave their own workspace; they can delete it instead.
- Existing protection against leaving/removing the last Administrator remains.

No other project features were intentionally removed.
